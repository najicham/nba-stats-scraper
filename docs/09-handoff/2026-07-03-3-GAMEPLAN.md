the real BB stream. With a trustworthy p_win:

  flat (HR unchanged; the UNDER win-curve is flat above edge 4 so Kelly ≈ capped-flat). If confirmed,
  **adopt edge-proportional UNDER staking** (stake = edge/5, 0.5-3u) and stop. Don't chase full Kelly.
