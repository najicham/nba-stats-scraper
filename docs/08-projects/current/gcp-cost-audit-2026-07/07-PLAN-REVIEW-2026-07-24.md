## The 10 lenses

### 1. Item-1 correctness (end-to-end contract) — ✅ SOLID
The applied push subscription matches the worker handler. `worker.py:752-765` decodes the Pub/Sub push envelope (`request.get_json()` → `message['data']` → `base64` → `json`). `predictions/coordinator/coordinator.py:3582-3605` publishes `json.dumps({**request_data, batch_id, correlation_id, prediction_run_mode, …})` — field names align with what the handler extracts. ### 2. Item-1 safety & reversibility — ✅ SOLID
Off-season: the schedulers that trigger the coordinator are not firing, so the subscription sits idle with no traffic. If a message did arrive it would deliver correctly. Fully reversible: `gcloud pubsub subscriptions delete prediction-request-prod`. Cost of an idle push subscription is ~zero.

### 3. Item-1 operational risk on opener (min=0 + push cold-start) — ⚠️ COVERED BY 4.9
The worker is `min-instances=0` (removed 2026-07-05). A push subscription against a cold worker can 503 and redeliver during ramp. Mitigant already in code: coordinator paces publishes ~50/sec for cold-start (`coordinator.py:3608`). Verified-not-assumed by the new **§4.9 pre-season smoke test** (via `prediction-request-dev`, not prod). This is the single highest-regret gap if unaddressed.
